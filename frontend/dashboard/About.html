About NeuroPlay

NeuroPlay is an AI-powered cognitive training and ADHD detection platform. We combine fun, engaging games with advanced analytics to track focus, memory, and attention while delivering transparent insights into cognitive health.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make early ADHD detection accurate, transparent, and accessible. By leveraging Explainable AI (XAI), NeuroPlay highlights behavioral factors influencing performance, and empowers schools, clinics, and families to monitor progress with confidence.

Core Features

🧠 ADHD Screening

Early, accurate, and objective ADHD detection powered by AI-driven analysis.

🎮 Cognitive Games

Personalized games designed to test and improve focus, memory, and attention.

📊 Progress Tracking

Monitor improvements over time with clear visual charts and weekly reports.

🔍 Explainable AI

Gain clarity on which behavioral factors influence results using XAI insights.
