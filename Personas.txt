ADHD Profiles

1. The Classic Inattentive Persona
Behavior: Represents individuals with generally slower processing speed and significant challenges with working memory.
Gameplay Data: Consistently slow reaction times in all games, combined with a high number of errors in tasks requiring memory (Memory game) and selective attention (Stroop game).

2. The Hyperfocused/Impulsive Persona
Behavior: Simulates the ability to hyperfocus on simple, stimulating tasks, leading to very fast performance, but also a tendency to make impulsive mistakes.
Gameplay Data: Very fast reaction times in the simple Reaction game, but a high error rate in the Memory and Stroop games due to impulsive clicks without sufficient thought.

3. The Variable Persona
Behavior: Models the performance inconsistency often seen in ADHD, where focus and speed can fluctuate significantly.
Gameplay Data: A wide, unpredictable range of scores across all metrics. This persona might perform well on one metric (e.g., reaction time) but poorly on another (e.g., error rate) in the same session.

4. The Executive Deficit Persona
Behavior: Represents a specific challenge with executive functions like task-switching and cognitive flexibility, which is heavily tested by the Stroop game.
Gameplay Data: Decent performance in the Reaction and Memory games, but a disproportionately high error rate and slow reaction time specifically in the Stroop game.

Neurotypical (Non-ADHD) Profiles

1. The Neurotypical High-Performer
Behavior: A baseline profile for a user who is consistently fast, accurate, and focused.
Gameplay Data: Low reaction times and low error rates across all three diagnosis games.

2. The Neurotypical Careful Persona
Behavior: Simulates a user who prioritizes accuracy over speed. They may be slower but are very deliberate in their actions.
Gameplay Data: High reaction times (overlapping with the "Inattentive" ADHD persona), but a very low error rate across all games, which is the key distinguishing factor.

3. The Neurotypical "Gamer" Persona
Behavior: Represents users who are accustomed to video games, resulting in very fast reaction times but a slightly higher tolerance for making occasional mistakes due to speed.
Gameplay Data: Very low reaction times (overlapping with the "Hyperfocused" ADHD persona), but only a moderately low error rate, distinguishing them from the more impulsive profile.
